Privacy

Your notes stay with you.

ChronoNote has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, and no servers of its own. What you type goes nowhere except your own OneDrive, if you turn on sync.

There are no advertising networks or third-party trackers anywhere: not on the website, in the web app or in the desktop app.

Where notes live

Desktop app: in your chosen notes folder (default Documents\Notes). They never leave your computer unless you back up or sync that folder yourself. Everything the app stores, and where.

Web app: in your browser's IndexedDB storage on your device.

OneDrive: when you turn on sync in the web app, notes go directly between your browser and the folder you pick in your own OneDrive (a personal or work account). They stay in your account.

The update check

The desktop app asks GitHub for latest.json when it starts and when you click Check for updates in Settings → About. The request only reads the latest version number; it sends no personal details, settings or note contents. You can turn off automatic checks in Settings → About.

OneDrive access

When you connect OneDrive in the web app, Microsoft asks you to allow three permissions.

PermissionWhy
Files.ReadWriteTo read and write the note files in the folder you select. ChronoNote only uses that folder.
offline_accessTo keep you signed in, so you don't have to reconnect on every visit.
User.ReadTo show which Microsoft account is connected.

The sign-in token is kept only in your browser's storage and is deleted when you sign out.

This website

The website sets no cookies and has no analytics. Fonts and scripts come from this site itself, so your browser contacts no other host. The only thing it stores is your choice of light or dark theme, in your own browser (localStorage), and only when you change it.

The download button and the GitHub links take you to GitHub, which has its own privacy statement.

Removing everything

Desktop app

  1. Uninstall ChronoNote via Windows Settings or Control Panel.
  2. Delete your notes folder (default Documents\Notes).
  3. Delete your settings and drafts in %APPDATA%\com.chrononote.app.
  4. Delete your logs in %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.chrononote.app.

Web app

  1. Click Sign out in Settings → Notes & Sync to remove your Microsoft tokens.
  2. Clear site data for app.chrononote.mariendegelder.nl in your browser settings to remove the local notes.
  3. If you used OneDrive sync, delete the notes folder from your OneDrive.
