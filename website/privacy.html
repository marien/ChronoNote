Privacy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

ChronoNote is built to keep your notes private and under your control. There are no accounts, no tracking scripts, and no telemetry services.

What is not collected

ChronoNote does not collect analytics, telemetry, or personal data. There are no advertising networks or third-party trackers on the website, in the web app, or in the desktop app. ChronoNote has no servers of its own: what you type goes nowhere except your own OneDrive, if you turn on sync.

Where notes live

Desktop app: Your notes live locally on your hard drive in your chosen notes folder (default Documents\Notes). They never leave your computer unless you back them up or sync that folder yourself.

Web app: Your notes live inside your browser's IndexedDB storage on your device.

OneDrive: If you turn on cloud sync in the web app, your notes sync directly between your browser and the folder you pick in your own OneDrive (a personal or work account). The notes stay in your own cloud account.

The update check

The desktop app queries GitHub for latest.json on startup and when you click Check for updates in Settings → About.

This request only checks the latest release version on GitHub. It sends no personal details, settings, or note contents. You can turn off automatic update checks in Settings → About.

OneDrive permissions and why

When you connect OneDrive in the web app, Microsoft asks for three permissions:

  • Files.ReadWrite: allows ChronoNote to read and write note files in the folder you select. The app only accesses that specific notes folder.
  • offline_access: keeps you signed in so you do not have to reconnect your Microsoft account on every visit.
  • User.Read: reads your basic profile to display which Microsoft account is connected.

The sign-in token is stored only in your browser storage and is deleted immediately when you sign out.

How to remove everything

To delete all ChronoNote data completely:

On desktop:

  1. Uninstall ChronoNote via Windows Settings or Control Panel.
  2. Delete your notes folder (default Documents\Notes).
  3. Delete your settings and drafts in %APPDATA%\com.chrononote.app.
  4. Delete your logs in %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.chrononote.app.

On the web app:

  1. Click Sign out in Settings → Notes & Sync to remove your Microsoft tokens.
  2. Clear site data for app.chrononote.mariendegelder.nl in your browser settings to remove all local notes.
  3. If you used OneDrive sync, delete the notes folder from your OneDrive.
