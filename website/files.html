Your files

ChronoNote saves your data in open, plain-text files on your own computer. You always own your notes, and you can open or edit them with any text editor.

Where your notes live

ChronoNote saves each day as one plain YYYY-MM-DD.txt file. You pick the folder in Settings → Notes & Sync. The default location is Documents\Notes in your user profile.

Hidden items in the notes folder

The app keeps a few hidden files and folders inside your notes folder:

  • .chrononote-session.json stores your open tabs and window session.
  • .chrononote-conflicts\ stores copies kept when a note changed on disk and in the app at the same time.
  • .agenda.json is an optional file written by your own calendar export and read by ChronoNote for calendar sync.
  • <name>.corrupt-<number> appears only if a settings or session file could not be read, set aside so the app could start.

Settings, drafts, and logs

ChronoNote keeps application data in standard Windows folders:

  • Settings live in %APPDATA%\com.chrononote.app\config.json.
  • Unsaved scratchpads live in %APPDATA%\com.chrononote.app\.scratchpads-drafts.json.
  • Logs live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.chrononote.app\logs\ (today only update.log).
  • The desktop application itself installs per user under %LOCALAPPDATA%\ChronoNote.

Backing up

To back up your notes, copy your notes folder. Because every note is a plain text file, any backup tool, external drive, or cloud service like OneDrive works without special tools.

Moving to a new PC

To move your notes to a new computer:

  1. Install ChronoNote on the new computer.
  2. Copy your notes folder to the new computer.
  3. Open Settings → Notes & Sync in ChronoNote and choose that folder.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling ChronoNote removes the application from %LOCALAPPDATA%\ChronoNote. The uninstaller does not delete your notes folder, your settings in %APPDATA%, or your logs in %LOCALAPPDATA%.

The web app's storage

In the web app at app.chrononote.mariendegelder.nl, notes stay in your browser's IndexedDB storage on that device. You can connect OneDrive to sync your notes with a folder you choose in your Microsoft account. Clearing site data in your browser removes local notes. Synced notes stay safe in OneDrive.
