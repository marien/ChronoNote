How it works

One week with ChronoNote

The team meeting in the morning, the soccer club or the church committee in the evening. Same notes, same marks, one list of what is still open. Every step opens in the real app.

Monday: type as it happens

ChronoNote opens on today's note. Give a meeting a title, underline it with ===, and type. A # makes a to-do, => @name hands it to someone. You never leave the keyboard or fill in a form.

Click a glyph to close an action, or press Ctrl+Space at the caret. Open work from every day and every part of your life collects in the action drawer, grouped by topic, by person or by due date.

Wednesday: the committee remembers itself

Use the same section title each time and section history shows every earlier meeting beside the note. Carry a line forward to tonight, or reuse last month's agenda with every topic open again.

Search every day at once. Filters like is:open, has:@Joan, tag:tournament and since: become chips, and results look exactly like the lines in your notes.

Friday: the whole week in one list

The week review lists every action from Monday to Sunday, from work and everything else: what is still open, what is late, and with one switch what got done. Close or forward straight from the list and start Monday clean.

Why text

Nothing between you and your notes.

No forms, no fields, no drag handles. Every line is text you can type over, cut, paste and undo. The glyphs are drawn on top; underneath, the note is exactly what you typed.

  • Edit without friction

    Change a to-do into done by typing v over the #, or click the glyph. Move a line, merge two, paste a list from an email: it is all just text.

  • Readable anywhere

    Each day is a .txt file. Notepad, VS Code, your phone's file viewer or a search tool all read it, and the marks were chosen so # call Sam still looks like a to-do.

  • Yours, without an account

    The desktop app keeps notes in a folder on your disk. The web app keeps them in your browser, or in your own OneDrive folder if you connect one. No tracking. Where everything is kept.

  • Nothing slips through

    Open actions follow you: a note with open work asks before it closes, the status bar counts what is late, and the week review shows what is still waiting.

Questions

Before you start

Is it really free?

Yes. ChronoNote is MIT-licensed open source. There is no paid tier and no account.

Is it only for work?

No. Anything with meetings, people and things to do fits: a soccer club committee, a church community, a renovation, your own week. Use one notes folder for all of it, and topics like (club) to keep things apart.

Where are my notes stored?

In the desktop app, in a folder you choose, one .txt file per day. In the web app, in your browser's storage, or in a OneDrive folder you connect. The demo stores nothing. More about your files.

Does it work on a Mac or on my phone?

The desktop app is for Windows. On a Mac, Linux or a phone, use the web app; it can sync with the same OneDrive folder as your PC.

Can I bring my existing notes?

Any text works. Drop a day's .txt file or a backup onto the window to import it; nothing is overwritten without asking. Importing notes.
