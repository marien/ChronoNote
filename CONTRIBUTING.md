# Contributing

ChronoNote is a solo personal project — I built it to fit how I personally
keep daily notes, and I maintain it on my own time.

- **Issues are welcome** — bug reports and feature ideas both. I read
  every one myself, but there's no fixed response time and no commitment
  to fix or implement anything in particular.
- **Pull requests are welcome too**, but please open an issue to discuss
  the change first, especially for anything beyond a small, obvious fix.
  Nothing merges without my own review, and I may decline changes that
  don't fit the project's direction even if they work correctly — that's
  not a reflection on the quality of the work.
- There's no formal style guide beyond matching the surrounding code by
  eye — no linter/formatter is configured. Most of the project's
  non-obvious decisions (and the reasoning behind them) are logged in
  `docs/CHANGELOG.md`, worth a search before re-proposing something that
  was already tried and reverted or reworked.
