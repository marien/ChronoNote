## 534. Names joined by a slash: `(@a/@b)`

**Status: released in v0.40.1.**

**Seen by Marien:** not yet (open check).

Marien noticed that `(@name, @name)` badges both names while `(@name/@name)` badged only the first. A `@` right after
`/` is not a name on purpose, so a URL path (`x.y/@dana`) stays plain text; that rule also caught the second name.
Now a `/` directly after a name is fine (`NAME_START` and `NAME_SLASH_CHAIN` in `grammar/symbols.ts`): `@a/@b/@c` is
three names, in parentheses or not, while `x.y/@a/@b` stays plain (its first `@a` is not a name, so neither is the
rest). Name suggestions open after `@a/@` too. Rows in `docs/spec/tokens.md` N6 and two in `suggestions.test.ts`.

## 535. The website's previews look like the app

**Status: released in v0.40.1.**

**Seen by Marien:** not yet (open check).

Marien noticed that the note previews on the website (the guide's marks table, the home page, the guide's

line in colour. The previews come from a small renderer of the site's own (`website/site.js`, `CN.render`), written

emphasis colour (also `! - item`); resolved lines muted to 72 % (a resolved topic with an open action after its arrow:
only the topic); `@name` badges in the app's badge colour and weight, with names joined by `/` (§534) and Unicode
names; consequence topics (`=> o`), `# - item`, the dimmed escape `\`, and a section underline as the app's double rule
