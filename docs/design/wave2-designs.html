F1 · Calendar sync adds new meetings by itself

Today a note that already has text is never changed without the review dialog. The proposal: changes that cannot lose or move anything you wrote happen on their own; everything else keeps the dot and the review.

The caret stays on its text and the view does not jump (the rule for outside changes, §469 and §477). The status bar says Added from calendar: Design review; a click goes to the section. It happens at the moments the dot is checked today: on a tab switch, when the window gets focus, and a short pause after typing. Never in the middle of a keystroke.

1.1The setting "Add new meetings automatically" (under Calendar): on or off by default?

It only exists while calendar sync itself is switched on. Off gives exactly today's behaviour.

1.2A cancelled meeting whose section is still empty

Nothing can be lost: the section holds only its title. Removing it keeps the day in step with the calendar without a dialog.

1.3A meeting that moved to another time

Moving a section moves text you may be reading or typing in. I would leave that to you, as now.

1.4You delete a section that was added automatically. The meeting is still in the calendar.

Without a rule the next check adds it again. Recommended: the app remembers, for that day, the titles you removed (in the folder's session file, never in the note) and leaves them out. "Sync calendar for this day" by hand still offers them.

1.5Is the added section something Ctrl+Z takes back?

Recommended: no, like every other change from outside (decision D1 of the editor review). Otherwise a Ctrl+Z meant for your last word could remove a meeting instead.

F2 · Copy a meeting as minutes

One command on the section the caret is in. It puts rich text (for Outlook and Teams) and plain text on the clipboard. The note is not changed.

Design review, Monday 16 November 2026

  • Vendor choice: we go with Acme

  • Send draft (contract): Marien, by Fri 20 Nov

  • Prepares the demo: Dana, by Tue 24 Nov
Still open
  • Budget 2027
  • Hiring plan (not discussed)
Remember
  • No meeting next week

How each line kind is read:

  • Decisions: a plain => text with the line it follows as its subject. A => that starts an action or names a person is an action.
  • Actions: every open action. The owner is the @name the line is delegated to, otherwise you. Due dates in words. The topic tag stays in brackets.
  • Still open: topics left to discuss (o) and postponed (,). Discussed topics appear only through their decisions.
  • Remember: the ! lines.
  • Left out: plain text lines, done and cancelled actions, forward links. A heading with nothing under it is left out too.

Headings and dates are in the UI language. The plain-text version is the same with - bullets and the headings on their own lines.

2.1The layout: four headings, in this order
2.2Your own actions: who is the owner in the minutes?

Recommended: a field "Your name in minutes" in Settings. While it is empty, your own actions are listed without an owner.

2.3Actions already done in the meeting (v)
2.4Plain text lines

They are often for yourself ("Sam was late"). Left out, nothing private leaves the note by accident.

2.5Where the command lives

Recommended: the command palette ("Copy as minutes") and the right-click menu of a section title, also in Peek. A shortcut later if you find yourself using it daily; with AltGr and the keys already taken, good ones are scarce.
