One list style for the week review and the Action Drawer

Both views answer the same question: what is still open, and where did it come from. They now share one way of drawing it: a quiet band per day (or per due group), every item one editor line with Section History's selection. The week review adds the meeting as a heading with a compact double rule; the drawer keeps showing the meeting only on the hovered or selected row. Hover a row to see it; click one to select it.

One date setting

"Tablabels" becomes "Datums in tabs en lijsten" and applies everywhere a note's date is a label: the top bar and phone tabs, Section History's date tabs, the Action Drawer's day bands and source label, Search's group headings, and the week review's day bands. The full date stays in the tooltip.

Day band

The note's date as the setting "Datums in tabs en lijsten" says (Leesbaar: "di 20 okt", "Gisteren", "Vandaag"; or the ISO date), quiet, with its counts on the right. It stays pinned at the top while you scroll, like the drawer's date heading does now. In the due view the bands are Te laat, Vandaag, Deze week, Later.

Meeting heading (week review only)

The section title in the note's font, semibold, with a compact double rule just under it: the note's identity, at a smaller scale. A meeting with nothing open is muted, with "alles voltooid" on the same line.

Items

One editor line each (13 px Cascadia Code, line height 1.6), the editor's glyphs, tree lines and pills. Headings and bands may be taller; items are not.

Selection from Section History

Hover: a light grey background. Selected: a darker grey with the line in semibold, rounded. The same in all three views and in Section History.

The drawer

No meeting headings: as today, the meeting (in the due view: date and meeting) shows right-aligned in grey on the hovered or selected row, and only when it fits. The uppercase bars with borders become the day bands; filter chips and scope buttons stay as they are.

Earlier weeks

"3 open uit eerdere weken, 1 te laat": two links, both with before: the Sunday before the week and is:open filled in. "3 open uit eerdere weken" opens the drawer on Alle bestanden (the middle pane); "1 te laat" opens it on Op deadline, where the overdue ones are at the top.
