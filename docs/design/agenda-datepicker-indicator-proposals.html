Redesigned Date Picker Indicators

⚠️ The Gestalt Proximity Problem (Option 1 Feedback)

Placing a floating dash at top: 2.5px put it ~5px from the row above and ~6px from the row below. In a tight calendar grid, the eye cannot instinctively tell if the dash is an underline for the day above or an overline for the day below.

✓ The Solution Strategy

To remove all ambiguity, the indicator must either: (1) directly hug the digits (sub-number underline), (2) span the cell width to define the cell roof, (3) join the bottom status bar, or (4) use a subtle cell surface tint.

Revised Proposals (Day Cells in Context)

1. Sub-Number Underline Recommended

A 9×1.5px bar sitting directly underneath the digits. 100% attached to its number; impossible to confuse with row above or below.
